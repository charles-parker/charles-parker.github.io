I love beer! And making things from scratch always makes it taste better. 

I started by trying to make ginger beer during the pandemic. My first batch didn't go quite to plan (my growler exploded, sending shards of glass everywhere, and marred our Labyrinth movie poster. Sorry, David Bowie). I have learned a lot since then, reading great books on the history of beer and homebrewing. 

I am currently brewing a Wheat bier and I am excited to learn how to make a fresh hop IPA that I enjoy.
