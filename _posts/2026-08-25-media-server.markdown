# Emby Home Media Server Project 
Project started July 2026

I love movies. I remember growing up and going to my local Blockbuster and had the pleasure of going to the Naro Cinema when I was stationed in Portsmouth, Virginia. Movie Madness is my favorite place in Portland and its mission inspired to create and preserve my movie collection. 

Currently, the media server is running on Fedora and I changed my OS to Mint to practice using Linux and terminal. I am currently in process of preserving my DVD collection. My next goal is to scan in my VHS collection. I love finding old movies, especially b rated horror movies and sharing them with my friends and family.
