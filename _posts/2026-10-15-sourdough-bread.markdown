I just love working with fermentation and sourdough is no exception! It took a lot of figuring out and failed started, before figuring out the right type and mixture of flour plus the right amount of water to get a successful starter going. I now have a 3 month old starter as of November 10th and have made wonderful boules and sandwhich loafs.
